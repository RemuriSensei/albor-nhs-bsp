About Outfit 379

BOY SCOUTS OF THE PHILIPPINES
ALBOR NHS SENIOR SCOUTS

Senior Scouts Outfit 379 of Albor National High School provides progressive, value-based character development and experiential outdoor programs. Guided by our motto "Laging Handa", we prepare young minds to lead with integrity, serve with compassion, and meet everyday challenges with honor.

Duty to God & Country

Instilling spiritual devotion, civic awareness, loyalty, and active love for the Philippines.

Responsibility & Honor

Fostering personal accountability, self-control, and honor in daily academic and personal life.

Service to Others

Dedicated to helping fellow citizens, assisting local communities, and embodying the daily good turn.

Respect & Cooperation

Promoting unity, respecting diversity, and building strong bonds through mutual support.

Leadership & Teamwork

Training future youth leaders through patrol system dynamics, peer guidance, and collaborative tasks.

Laging Handa (Preparedness)

Living by the motto "Laging Handa" — equipping scouts with first aid, survival, and practical problem-solving skills.

Student Advantages

WHY JOIN OUTFIT 379?

Scouting equips Albor National High School students with transformative experiences that extend far beyond the classroom.

Leadership Development

Develop confidence, decision-making, communication, and responsibility as a student leader in Senior Scouting.

Outdoor Adventure

Discover outdoor activities, camping, and nature exploration through properly supervised Outfit 379 programs.

Practical Life Skills

Learn useful skills such as preparedness, problem-solving, and first-aid awareness through hands-on training.

Brotherhood & Friendship

Build meaningful lifelong friendships and learn how to collaborate effectively within Senior Scout patrols.

Community Service

Take part in approved civic projects that help the school and local Albor community thrive.

Personal Growth

Develop discipline, resilience, confidence, and a positive attitude toward lifelong service.

Privacy Notice & Protection

1. Purpose of Information Collection: Information requested in the recruitment form (Name, Grade, Section, Age, and Facebook Profile Name) is solely used for evaluating eligibility for membership in the Boy Scouts of the Philippines Outfit 379 at Albor National High School.

2. Data Processing & Privacy Security: This website is a client-side portal. It does NOT collect, store, or transmit personal details to any database or external cloud server. All information remains on your local device until you choose to copy and send it via Facebook Messenger.

3. Review Authorization: Only designated ANHS Scouting Advisers and Coordinators of Outfit 379 are authorized to review membership applications received via official channels.

4. Minor Student Protection: In compliance with school guidelines and Philippine Data Privacy principles, minor students are required to secure parental or guardian consent before participating in official scouting activities and encampments.

5. Verification & Contact: For inquiries regarding student privacy or recruitment procedures, please contact the authorized school scouting coordinator via our official Messenger channel.
